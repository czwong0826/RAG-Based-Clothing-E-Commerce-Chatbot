WASHING AND MAINTENANCE GUIDE

Overview
Proper washing and maintenance help clothing last longer, maintain color, and preserve fabric quality.

Cotton

Washing:
- Machine wash in cold or warm water.
- Use mild detergent.

Drying:
- Tumble dry on low heat or air dry.

Tips:
- Wash dark colors separately.
- Cotton may shrink under high heat.

Polyester

Washing:
- Machine wash with cold or warm water.

Drying:
- Low heat drying recommended.

Tips:
- Avoid excessive heat.
- Resistant to wrinkles and shrinking.

Wool

Washing:
- Hand wash or use wool cycle.
- Use wool-specific detergent.

Drying:
- Lay flat to dry.

Tips:
- Do not wring.
- Avoid high temperatures.

Denim

Washing:
- Turn garments inside out.
- Wash in cold water.

Drying:
- Air dry when possible.

Tips:
- Wash less frequently to preserve color.
- Separate from light-colored garments.

Linen

Washing:
- Gentle machine wash or hand wash.

Drying:
- Air dry preferred.

Tips:
- Iron while slightly damp.
- Natural wrinkles are normal.

Silk

Washing:
- Hand wash with gentle detergent or dry clean.

Drying:
- Air dry away from direct sunlight.

Tips:
- Do not bleach.
- Avoid strong detergents.

General Care Instructions

Color Protection:
- Wash dark and light colors separately.
- Turn garments inside out before washing.

Stain Treatment:
- Treat stains as soon as possible.
- Test stain removers on a small hidden area first.

Storage:
- Store in a cool, dry place.
- Use hangers for jackets and shirts.
- Fold knitwear and sweaters to prevent stretching.

Ironing Guidelines

Low Heat:
- Polyester
- Nylon

Medium Heat:
- Cotton blends

High Heat:
- Cotton
- Linen

Avoid Ironing Directly:
- Printed graphics
- Embroidery
- Decorative elements

Common Care Symbols

Machine Wash:
- Garment can be machine washed.

Hand Wash:
- Hand washing recommended.

Do Not Bleach:
- Bleach may damage fabric.

Tumble Dry Low:
- Dry using low heat only.

Do Not Tumble Dry:
- Air drying recommended.

Dry Clean Only:
- Professional cleaning required.

Customer Support Guidance

If care instructions on the product label differ from general recommendations, always follow the product label instructions.

Product-specific care instructions take priority over general fabric care guidance.
