SIZE RECOMMENDATION GUIDE

Overview
Selecting the correct size improves comfort, appearance, and overall customer satisfaction.

Required Measurements

For Tops:
- Chest circumference
- Shoulder width
- Height
- Weight

For Bottoms:
- Waist circumference
- Hip circumference
- Inseam length

Recommendation Rules

Rule 1:
If measurements fall between two sizes, recommend the larger size for a relaxed fit.

Rule 2:
For slim-fit products, recommend the larger size if the customer prefers comfort.

Rule 3:
For oversized products, customers may choose their normal size.

Rule 4:
Always prioritize chest measurements for shirts, jackets, and outerwear.

Rule 5:
Always prioritize waist measurements for pants and shorts.

Fit Definitions

Slim Fit:
- Closer to the body.
- Modern appearance.

Regular Fit:
- Balanced comfort and appearance.

Relaxed Fit:
- Looser fit with more room.

Oversized Fit:
- Intentionally larger silhouette.

When Information Is Missing

If the customer does not provide measurements:
1. Ask for height and weight.
2. Ask for usual clothing size.
3. Ask for preferred fit (slim, regular, relaxed, oversized).

Important Note

Size recommendations are estimates.
Different brands and product categories may have different sizing standards.
Always refer to the specific product size chart when available.
