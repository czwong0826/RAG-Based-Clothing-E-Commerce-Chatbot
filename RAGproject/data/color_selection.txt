COLOR SELECTION GUIDE

Overview
Choosing the right color can improve appearance, match personal style, and suit different occasions.

Skin Tone Recommendations

1. Warm Skin Tone
Characteristics:
- Yellow, golden, or peach undertones.
- Gold jewelry typically looks better than silver.

Recommended Colors:
- Beige
- Cream
- Camel
- Olive green
- Mustard yellow
- Coral
- Warm red
- Brown

Colors to Use Carefully:
- Icy blue
- Cool gray
- Pure white

2. Cool Skin Tone
Characteristics:
- Pink, red, or blue undertones.
- Silver jewelry typically looks better than gold.

Recommended Colors:
- Navy
- Emerald
- Sapphire blue
- Lavender
- Burgundy
- Cool gray
- Pure white
- Black

Colors to Use Carefully:
- Orange
- Mustard yellow
- Yellow-green

3. Neutral Skin Tone
Characteristics:
- Mix of warm and cool undertones.

Recommended Colors:
- Most colors work well.
- Navy
- Teal
- Dusty pink
- Beige
- White
- Charcoal gray

Occasion-Based Recommendations

Casual Wear:
- Light blue
- White
- Gray
- Olive
- Pastel colors

Business Wear:
- Navy
- Black
- Charcoal gray
- White
- Light blue

Formal Events:
- Black
- Navy
- Burgundy
- Emerald green

Seasonal Recommendations

Spring:
- Light pink
- Mint green
- Sky blue
- Lavender

Summer:
- White
- Beige
- Light blue
- Coral

Autumn:
- Brown
- Olive
- Rust
- Mustard

Winter:
- Black
- Navy
- Dark green
- Burgundy

General Color Matching Rules

- Neutral colors pair well with almost any color.
- Avoid combining too many bright colors in one outfit.
- Use one dominant color and one or two accent colors.
- Black and white are versatile and suitable for most occasions.

When customers are unsure, recommend:
- Black
- Navy
- White
- Gray

These colors are timeless, versatile, and easy to match.
